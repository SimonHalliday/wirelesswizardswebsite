How They're Made

Printed To Order.
UK Based. Low Waste.

We didn't want boxes of unsold shirts in the workshop, and we didn't want to ship cheap blanks halfway round the world. Our shirts are made by Teemill, a print-on-demand company on the Isle of Wight, and here's why we picked them.

🇬🇧
Printed on the Isle of Wight
Every shirt is printed, packed and posted from Teemill's factory in the UK, so your order isn't crossing an ocean to reach you.
🖨️
Made when you order it
Nothing is printed until it's bought. No stock piles, no overruns, no landfill of last season's designs.
🌱
Organic cotton
The blanks are 100% certified organic cotton, grown without synthetic pesticides and made in a certified factory that runs on wind and solar power.
☀️
Renewable-powered printing
The UK print facility runs on renewable energy, and the water-based inks are vegan-friendly.
♻️
Designed to come back
When a shirt is worn out, scan the QR code on the label to send it back for free. It gets remade into new products, and you get money off the next one.
📦
Plastic-free packaging
Shipped in paper packaging, not poly bags. Teemill is a certified B Corp, which means the whole business is audited on this stuff, not just the marketing.

We're an RF company, not a clothing brand — we've simply picked a supplier whose approach we're happy to put our name on. Full details of Teemill's process are at teemill.com.
